# Chapter 1 — About FloodWatch and This Guide

## 1.1 Welcome to FloodWatch

FloodWatch is a community flood-reporting and awareness app for **Sta. Ana, Taytay, Rizal**. It brings together information that can help residents understand reported flood conditions in the service area.

In the Android app, a resident can:

- View a Dashboard with a map, weather information, active alerts, and a flood-risk summary.
- See map markers for flood reports that an administrator has verified and that were submitted within the previous 24 hours.
- Submit a flood report with a detected location, flood-depth selection, vehicle guidance, elevation information, and a photo taken through the app.
- Read active flood alerts posted by an administrator.
- View verified community flood reports.
- Track personal reports and filter them by status.
- Withdraw an eligible report while it is still Pending.
- Manage profile information, a profile photo, an emergency contact, a Home Zone address, and notification preferences.

FloodWatch also has a separate browser-based panel for an authorized administrator. The administrator can review submitted reports, categorize them, post flood alerts, deactivate alerts, and manage eligible report-history items. The admin panel is not part of the Android app.

## 1.2 How community reports are used

A report submitted by a resident starts with a Pending status. It does not immediately become a verified report. An authorized administrator reviews the submission and can categorize it as Verified, Dismissed, Invalid Image, or Invalid Information.

A verified report can appear in resident-facing areas such as the verified-report list. If it was created within the previous 24 hours, it can also appear as a marker on the Dashboard map and can influence the Dashboard risk card.

The Dashboard risk card uses the **newest verified report from the previous 24 hours**. It does not use the most severe older report, and it does not confirm conditions along an entire road or route.

> **Note:** A resident can use **My Reports** to check the current status of a submitted report. Report-status notifications may also be available when notification access is enabled.

## 1.3 Who this guide is for

Most chapters are for residents and other regular authenticated users of the FloodWatch Android app. They explain account access, Dashboard information, flood reporting, alerts, report history, profile settings, privacy, and troubleshooting.

**Administrator Guide** is for authorized administrators who use the separate web panel. Administrator access is restricted. This guide does not provide an administrator email address, password, or instructions for bypassing that restriction.

The guide uses the same visible labels as the app. For example, it refers to **Dashboard**, **Report**, **Alerts**, **My Reports**, and **Profile** exactly as they appear in the bottom navigation. When an exact message matters, the message is shown in bold.

## 1.4 What FloodWatch does not do

FloodWatch supports awareness and community reporting. It is not an official emergency service.

The current app does not provide:

- Emergency dispatch or an automatic rescue request.
- An in-app emergency hotline.
- Chat or direct messaging with an administrator.
- Route planning or turn-by-turn navigation.
- Resident editing of a report after it has been submitted.
- A guarantee that a road marked **PASSABLE**, **STABLE**, or **NORMAL** is safe at the time you travel.

The Emergency Contact option opens the device's dialer or messaging app for the saved contact. FloodWatch does not place the call or send the message itself.

> **Warning:** Never rely on FloodWatch alone during an emergency. Follow official government warnings, emergency responders, road closures, and evacuation instructions. Do not enter floodwater or move into danger to take a report photo.

## 1.5 How to use this guide

Use the guide in the following order if you are new to FloodWatch:

1. Read **Getting Started: Access, Permissions, and Navigation** to understand access requirements, permissions, and the five main navigation tabs.
2. Follow **Account, Sign-up, Login, and Password Recovery** to create an account, verify the email address, log in, or recover a password.
3. Read **Dashboard, Map, Weather, and Risk Information** before relying on Dashboard markers, alert severity, the evacuation-status label, or the risk card.
4. Read **Creating and Submitting a Flood Report** and **Safety, Privacy, and Responsible Use** before submitting a flood report. These sections explain both the reporting workflow and the safety/privacy considerations for a photo with location metadata.
5. Use **Flood Alerts and Verified Reports**, **My Reports: Status, Filters, and Withdrawal**, **Report-status Notifications and Badge**, and **Profile, Preferences, Emergency Contact, and Home Zone** to understand alerts, report status, notifications, and settings.
6. Use **Troubleshooting and Frequently Asked Questions** when a screen does not load, a permission is denied, or an action fails.
7. Use **Administrator Guide** only if you are an authorized administrator working in the separate web panel.

**Expected result:** You know which part of the guide contains the instructions for your task, and you understand that app information must be used together with official safety information.

## 1.6 Conventions used in this guide

Numbered lists show actions that should be completed in order. Bold text identifies visible labels, status names, and messages. A **Note** adds useful context. A **Warning** identifies a safety, privacy, or account-security concern.

An **Expected result** appears after every important workflow. It describes what the app should show or do when the steps succeed. If the result is different, use **Troubleshooting and Frequently Asked Questions**.

Android system permission dialogs may look different depending on the Android version and device manufacturer. The requested purpose remains the same: location supports position and report details, camera access supports report photos, and notification access supports report-status updates.
